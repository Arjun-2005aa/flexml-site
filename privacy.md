# FlexML Privacy Policy

_Effective: 14 November 2026_

FlexML ("the app") is made by Arjun K ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use FlexML on iPhone or iPad.

**In short: we don't collect any data.** FlexML has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything is processed on your device. Data leaves your device only when **you** connect FlexML to a computer, robot or other device that **you** choose.

## What the app uses, and where it goes

| Information | How FlexML uses it | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | Runs the machine-learning model you select on live camera frames | Stays on your device, unless you record a session or stream to a device you connected |
| **Motion and AR data** (camera position, motion sensors, LiDAR depth, point clouds) | Anchors objects in 3D, measures distances and builds maps | Stays on your device, unless you save and export a Space or stream it to a device you connected |
| **Recordings** (video, detections, camera path) | Created only when you tap Record | Saved inside the app on your device. They leave it only if you share or export them, or save a video to Photos |
| **Models you import** | Run on your device with Apple's Core ML | Stored inside the app on your device |
| **Settings** (e.g. your computer's address, display preferences) | Remembered between launches | Stored on your device |
| **Local network** | Connects to the computer or robot you choose and finds the FlexML companion on your Wi-Fi | Sent only to that device on your network |
| **Bluetooth** | Sends results to, and receives commands from, a microcontroller you pair (only when you turn on Bluetooth output) | Sent only to that device |

We do not receive any of this information. We have no servers that the app talks to.

## Data you send to your own devices

When you connect FlexML to a computer, robot or microcontroller, the app sends the results you choose (for example detections, camera position, 3D objects or the map) to that device. That device is under your control, and what happens to the data there is up to you and the software you run on it, including our open-source companion software, which also does not send data to us.

## Permissions

iOS asks for permission before FlexML can use the camera, the local network, Bluetooth or save to your photo library. FlexML asks only when you use a feature that needs it. You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

## Third parties

FlexML includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. If you import files through the Files app or iCloud Drive, or share recordings, Apple's privacy policy applies to those services.

## Children

FlexML is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Deleting your data

All data is stored on your device. Delete recordings, Spaces and models in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, we'll say so here and in the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
